# EKE Reviewer Requirements
# Reviewer Requirements Baseline v0.1

## Status

DESIGNED — REVIEWER REQUIREMENTS BASELINE

This document defines the initial requirements boundary for reviewers
participating in an EKE Open Review.

It does not constitute evidence that any reviewer has been verified,
credentialed, accredited, or authorized.

## 1. Reviewer Identity

Every reviewer participating in a review must have a unique Reviewer ID.

Reviewer identity records should distinguish between:

- Public Reviewer ID
- Legal identity
- Institutional affiliation
- Professional role
- Professional identifier where available

Private identity information must not be exposed merely because a
review record is public.

## 2. Independence

Where a review is designated independent, the reviewer must declare
the independence conditions applicable to the review.

Independence must not be inferred merely from the label "independent".

The review record should identify:

- Relevant relationships
- Financial conflicts
- Institutional conflicts
- Author or contributor relationships
- Other material conflicts

## 3. Conflict of Interest

A reviewer must disclose material conflicts of interest relevant to
the review.

Possible declarations include:

- NONE_DECLARED
- DECLARED
- UNDER_REVIEW

A conflict declaration does not automatically invalidate a review.
Its effect must be determined according to the applicable review policy.

## 4. Competence

The review record should identify the technical or methodological
competence relevant to the declared review level.

Competence evidence may include:

- Relevant experience
- Technical expertise
- Publications
- Professional identifiers
- Previous review activity
- Demonstrated reproduction capability

The presence of a credential does not by itself establish review quality.

## 5. Review Level

Reviewer requirements must correspond to the applicable review level:

R0 — Document Review

R1 — Technical Review

R2 — Independent Reproduction

R3 — Multi-Implementation Verification

R4 — Formal / Mathematical / Security Review

R5 — Institutional / Standards-Level Review

Review level does not itself imply certification authority.

## 6. Evidence Handling

The reviewer must remain within the declared evidence boundary.

Evidence obtained outside the declared boundary must be explicitly
identified before being used as review evidence.

Material evidence should remain traceable to its source.

## 7. AI Participation

If AI systems participate in the review, the reviewer must disclose:

- AI usage
- System/provider
- Model/version where available
- Purpose
- Decision authority
- Reviewer verification of AI-generated outputs

AI assistance must not silently become an independent human determination.

## 8. Attestation

A reviewer attestation must identify what the reviewer declares they:

- Examined
- Executed
- Reproduced
- Observed
- Determined

An attestation must remain bounded by the evidence and procedures
actually available to the reviewer.

## 9. Reviewer Responsibility

The reviewer is responsible for accurately representing:

- Review scope
- Evidence used
- Procedures performed
- Results observed
- Limitations
- Conflicts
- AI participation

The reviewer must not represent unperformed procedures as completed.

## 10. Public Verifiability

Public review records should expose sufficient information to verify
the existence and integrity of the review without unnecessarily exposing
sensitive personal information.

Where implemented, public verification may use:

- Reviewer ID
- Institutional affiliation
- Attestation
- Signature
- Identity commitment
- Integrity metadata

## 11. Reviewer Status

Reviewer status must be explicitly represented.

Suggested states:

- APPLIED
- ACCEPTED
- ACTIVE
- COMPLETED
- SUSPENDED
- WITHDRAWN
- REVOKED

A reviewer status must not be inferred from the existence of a review.

## 12. Non-Claims

This baseline does not claim:

- reviewer accreditation
- professional certification
- institutional authorization
- independent verification
- scientific competence

unless separately established through identifiable evidence.

## 13. Change Control

Changes to reviewer requirements must be:

1. Explicitly identified.
2. Versioned.
3. Traceable.
4. Reviewed for impact.
5. Reflected in applicable review records.
